### Who I am

I am a final-year master's student in computer science and a teaching assistant at the [Dept. of Theoretical Computer Science, FIT CTU](https://fit.cvut.cz/en/faculty/organizational-structure/departments/533-department-of-theoretical-computer-science).
You can read more about my academic activities in the [Academia](/academia/) section.

I work as a software developer and analyst at <a href="https://www.target-technologie.cz/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Target Technologie</a>.
You can read about what I do at work on my <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/patrik-drbal/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LinkedIn profile</a>.

I am a proud member of the <a href="https://www.tek.fi/en/news-blogs/teekkari-culture-is-for-everyone-guide-to-teekkari-culture-now-in-english" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Teekkari culture</a>
and a former member of the <a href="https://www.tietokilta.fi/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Tietokilta</a> and <a href="https://www.inkubio.fi/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Inkubio</a> guilds.
I was one of the most active exchange students of the Autumn 2025 semester at Aalto University's School of Science, and I continue to spread Teekkari culture internationally through the [CHEESE](/me/communities/#teekkari-and-cheese) community.
