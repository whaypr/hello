### Teekkari and CHEESE

Teekkari is the name for a student of a technical field in Finland.

I first came into contact with this incredibly rich and welcoming community of Teekkaris during my exchange studies at Aalto University in 2025. During my stay I attended many events and gave a lot to the community, and the community gave even more to me. I became a proud Teekkari and will be one for the rest of my life – as the saying goes: *Once a Teekkari, always a Teekkari*.

Before leaving Finland to return to Czechia, a couple of friends and I initiated the creation of **CHEESE** – CHentral Eastern European Sitsit Enjoyers – an international community built on Finnish Teekkari student culture.
The point of CHEESE is to keep the friendships we made during our Teekkari adventures alive, to let our Teekkari selves be expressed, and to bring Teekkari tradition and culture into an international context, so that interested people from all over Europe can continue enjoying this community even once their days in Finland are behind them.

So far we have organised two international sitsits: one in Prague for about 90 people, the other in Vienna for about 60.
Our Telegram group has almost 200 members and keeps growing.

### diFAdlo

[DiFAdlo](https://www.instagram.com/difadlo_/) is the theatre club of the Faculty of Architecture at CTU, founded by my girlfriend Kristýna Dusílková and Jana Bušová.

So far I have:
- played a small role in an adaptation of Václav Havel's *Asanace* ([link](https://www.fa.cvut.cz/cs/galerie/ze-zivota-fa/143996-predstaveni-fakultniho-divadelniho-souboru-24-2-2026)),
- played one of the main roles in *Krizový štáb*, an original play by Kristýna and Jana,
- played a small role in a short play ([link](https://www.mlp.cz/cz/akce/vs-divadelni-soubory-comica-economica-difadlo/12692)) by Kristýna and Jana again,
- taken part in two public improv workshops and many improv sessions at our regular meetings.

After years of helping student theatre behind the scenes as a [light technician in high school](/me/communities/#light-technician-for-the-high-school-theatre), it is nice to experience it from the other side.

### Sincoolka

Sincoolka is the student club of the Sinkule dormitory.
The dorm has been my home for my whole university studies; over the years I got to know many of the students living here and eventually became an active member of the club.

For the club I:
- maintain the club's common room,
- act as club and network registrar,
- occasionally help prepare events and with internal club work, from installing network access points to cleaning the club's spaces,
- occasionally attend board meetings,
- created and maintain the emojis on the club's Discord server.
