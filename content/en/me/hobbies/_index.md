**Tinkering**

I like to tinker and to make things.

Often that means tinkering on my computer – enhancing my Linux distro, trying out new tools and technologies, or playing with familiar ones – but it doesn't stop there.

My biggest project is my *opiskelijahaalarit*, the Finnish student overalls, onto which I sew patches and attach all kinds of things. One side project was sewing a custom hood for them from scratch!

Sometimes I handcraft small gifts for friends, family or community events – I like making origami for them, engraving things in wood, or building small creative software projects.

When I was younger I built a lot of stuff from Lego and its Mindstorms NXT robotics set.
Now I sometimes build simple things with a Raspberry Pi or an Arduino.

For that I sometimes visit public workshops such as the [NTK polytechnic workshop](https://www.techlib.cz/en/85017-polytechnic-workshop). During my stay in Finland I was a happy user of [Aalto's Design Factory](https://designfactory.aalto.fi/).

**Sports**

My favourite sport is basketball.
I started playing when I was about 12; sometimes I play more regularly, sometimes less, but I have been playing ever since. I have always played just for fun and never trained much, though together with a friend I led an after-school club for two school years, as part of our [other activities](/me/communities/#basketball) of encouraging local people to play basketball.

Besides basketball I go to the gym regularly, and I like cycling, ice skating and swimming.
From time to time I also pack a big backpack and go hiking – I have hiked in Czechia, Romania, Bulgaria, North Macedonia and Poland.

---

**Dancing**

I took a dance course when I was 15. I liked it, but I didn't dance much after that.
In 2026 I started dancing more actively with my girlfriend: we regularly attend the ESN open classes in ballroom dancing, and we dance swing – we took balboa lessons together, and now she gives me private lindy hop lessons. From time to time we take our new moves out to [social dancing at NTK](https://swingbusters.cz/en/5007/swing-dance-party-and-open-class-at-ntk).

**Playing the ukulele**

I got a ukulele as a gift a couple of years back.
I have never been to a music school, but I enjoy teaching myself and practising on my own.
I'm not any good, but it is a great way for me to relax – I just grab the ukulele and play a bit from time to time.
